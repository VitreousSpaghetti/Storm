# Testi del menu GUIDE di storm (in inglese, come lo schermo). Li legge script/prepara.py, che
# li misura col font e scrive testi.h: una riga troppo lunga ferma la compilazione con l'errore.
# [VOCE DEL MENU] poi le righe della pagina (al massimo 9, larghe al massimo 186 pixel).
# Righe che iniziano con > = tasti: le parole dei tasti diventano gialle.
# Simboli: {UD} su/giu', {LR} sinistra/destra, {X} croce, {Q} quadrato, {T} triangolo, {C} cerchio.
# Righe che iniziano con # = commenti.
# [[CONFERMA]] e [[ATTIVO]]: pagine della voce TUTORIAL (prima di partire, tutorial in corso).
# [[TUTORIAL]]: i passi, separati da ~. Righe = cosa fare, righe + cosa si sente (2 + 2, larghe al
# massimo 248 pixel: stanno in testata). Le condizioni dei passi sono in main.c (passi[]), nello
# stesso ordine: se si aggiunge o toglie un passo va cambiato anche li'.

[SIGNAL PATH]
THE SOUND FLOWS LEFT TO
RIGHT: SOURCES MAKE THE
TONE, SHAPE FILTERS IT,
LAYERS ADD VOICES, SPACE
PUTS IT IN A ROOM.
MOTION TURNS KNOBS BY
ITSELF. A MACRO TURNS
MANY KNOBS AT ONCE.

[KEYS]
> PAD 1 START: MUTE (FADE)
> PAD 1 SELECT TAP: PLAY
> PAD 1 SELECT HOLD: OSC B
> PAD 2 START TAP: FREEZE
> PAD 2 START HOLD: SCENES
> PAD 2 SELECT HOLD: MACROS
> START+SELECT STORM GUIDE
> PAD 1 START+{X} 2 S: LOCK
> START+R1 L1: TUTORIAL

[SOURCES]
ONE OSCILLATOR, 4 WAVES:
SINE, SQUARE, SAW, SUB.
OSC B: FM AND AM. FOLD
BENDS THE WAVE. TEXTURE:
THUMP ON PULSE 1, WIND,
PEDAL (SUB OR LAYERS STAY
ON THE ROOT), FINE TUNE.
> {X} OSC {Q} GRAIN {T} NOTE
> SELECT OSC B  +{X} TEXTURE

[SHAPE]
A FILTER. CUTOFF: HOW
BRIGHT. RES: IT SINGS AT
THE CUTOFF. DRIVE: GRIT.
MODE: LOW, BAND OR HIGH
PASS (LP BP HP).
> PAD 1, NO KEYS HELD:
> {UD} CUTOFF {LR} RES
> L1R1 MODE L2R2 DRIVE

[LAYERS]
FOUR MORE VOICES, ONE
OCTAVE UP, A BIT OUT OF
TUNE: THEY BEAT, SHIMMER.
BELLS: THE PS1 SHAKES
EACH VOICE (METAL GLINTS).
COLOR: FROM DARK TO AIR.
CHORD: WHICH NOTES.
> PAD 1: HOLD {C} LAYERS
> PAD 2: L1R1 LEVEL

[MOTION]
3 HANDS TURN KNOBS FOR
YOU, NEVER IN TIME: SIN,
TRI, BREATH, RANDOM, NOISE.
DEPTH: HOW MUCH. DEST: A
KNOB, PITCH, PAN, A MACRO,
OR DEPTH OR RATE OF
ANOTHER HAND.
> PAD 2: HOLD {X} {Q} {T}
> MOD A B C  START FREEZE

[SEQ]
THE NOTE MOVES BY ITSELF
ON A SCALE: ONE STEP
EVERY 1-16 BEATS. MUTATE:
HOW OFTEN IT CHANGES.
PULSE: TWO RHYTHMS. A MOD
WITH WAVE PULSE1 OR 2.
> PAD 2: HOLD SELECT +
> {X} SEQ {Q} PULSE {T} CLOCK
> {T}: L1R1 NUDGE L2R2 TAP

[PLAY]
SELECT ON PAD 1: PLAY ON
OR OFF. THE D-PAD PLAYS:
> {UD} UP SWELL, DOWN SINK
> {LR} NOTE ON THE SCALE
> L1R1 OCTAVE L2R2 CHORD
SWELL AND SINK PUSH
MACRO 1 AND 2 WHILE HELD.

[SPACE]
WIDE SPREADS THE LAYERS
LEFT AND RIGHT (PAST 50:
WIDER THAN THE SPEAKERS).
REVERB IS THE ROOM, TAIL
HOW LONG IT RINGS, DAMP
MAKES IT DARKER.
TAIL 99 = HOLD: IT STAYS.
> PAD 2: HOLD {C} SPACE
> PAD 2: {LR} REVERB

[MACROS]
ONE KNOB TURNS 4 KNOBS,
EACH UP OR DOWN BY ITS
OWN AMOUNT (-9 TO +9).
RISE DEEP UNREST HAZE.
A MOD CAN TURN A MACRO:
THE DRONE PLAYS ITSELF.
> PAD 2: HOLD SELECT
> SELECT+{C}: EDIT

[VISUAL]
A STORM AT SEA, FROM THE
BOW OF A FISHING BOAT.
EVERY KNOB MOVES IT: NOISE
RAIN, CUT LIGHT, FM BOLTS,
FOLD FOAM, SUB SWELL,
MACROS THE HURRICANE.
HOLD A PAGE: VALUES SHOW.
> START+SELECT TAP: STORM
> HOLD 1 S: THIS GUIDE

[TUTORIAL]
BUILDS A DRONE FROM A
PLAIN SINE, ONE STEP AT
A TIME, AND WAITS FOR YOU.
START+R1 SKIPS A STEP,
START+L1 GOES BACK.
> {X} START

[RESET SOUND]
PUTS EVERY KNOB, MACRO
AND MOD BACK TO THE
START SOUND.
> {X} RESET

[SAVE PATCH]
KEEPS YOUR SOUND ON THE
MEMORY CARD IN PORT 1.
> {LR} SLOT  {X} SAVE

[LOAD PATCH]
BRINGS BACK A SOUND,
GLIDING (MORPH TIME).
LIVE: HOLD PAD 2 START.
> {LR} SLOT  {X} LOAD

[CLOSE]
CLOSES THE GUIDE.
THE SOUND NEVER STOPPED.
> {X} OR {T}

[[CONFERMA]]
THE SOUND RESTARTS FROM
A PLAIN SINE.
> {X} YES  {T} NO

[[ATTIVO]]
THE TUTORIAL IS RUNNING.
QUIT: THE SOUND STAYS
AS IT IS NOW.
> {X} QUIT TUTORIAL
> {T} BACK TO THE GUIDE
